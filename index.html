Over a career span­ning more than 26 years, Abu Naser has become one of the prin­ci­pal archi­tects of modern retail in Bangla­desh. As the oper­a­tional and com­mer­cial lead behind ACI Logis­tics Lim­ited (Shwapno), he guided the busi­ness from market chal­lenger to market leader, rais­ing the brand from No. 3 to No. 1 as rec­og­nized by Bangla­desh Brand Forum.

His lead­er­ship com­bines market intel­li­gence, real estate fore­sight and dis­ci­plined P&L gov­er­nance. Under his direc­tor­ship, monthly national sales grew by 1,140% — from BDT 25 Crore to BDT 310 Crore — while the net­work expanded at a sus­tained pace of 30 new stores a month for four con­sec­u­tive months, and the final year deliv­ered 35% sales growth and 30% foot­fall growth.

As Chief Busi­ness Offi­cer, he now leads Shwapno’s Big Box Retail Strat­egy across Volume and Ware­house Retail, the roll­out of the Net­work-Led Model and Man­aged Retail Points, and deliv­ery of Vision 2030 — the roadmap to a BDT 3,500 Crore com­pany port­fo­lio that he devel­oped as Direc­tor Oper­a­tions.

Ear­lier, at OTOBI Ltd, he directed regional oper­a­tions across China, India, Thai­land and Malaysia, grow­ing annual com­pany sales from BDT 95 Crore to BDT 554 Crore. His foun­da­tion was built in FMCG field sales and modern trade, where he was named Best Ter­ri­tory Offi­cer of the Year nation­wide.
