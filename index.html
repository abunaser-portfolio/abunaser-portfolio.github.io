Over a career spanning more than 26 years, Abu Naser has become one of the principal architects of modern retail in Bangladesh. As the operational and commercial lead behind ACI Logistics Limited (Shwapno), he guided the business from market challenger to market leader, raising the brand from No. 3 to No. 1 as recognized by Bangladesh Brand Forum.

His leadership combines market intelligence, real estate foresight and disciplined P&L governance. Under his directorship, monthly national sales grew by 1,140% — from BDT 25 Crore to BDT 310 Crore — while the network expanded at a sustained pace of 30 new stores a month for four consecutive months, and the final year delivered 35% sales growth and 30% footfall growth.

As Chief Business Officer, he now leads Shwapno’s Big Box Retail Strategy across Volume and Warehouse Retail, the rollout of the Network-Led Model and Managed Retail Points, and delivery of Vision 2030 — the roadmap to a BDT 3,500 Crore company portfolio that he developed as Director Operations.

Earlier, at OTOBI Ltd, he directed regional operations across China, India, Thailand and Malaysia, growing annual company sales from BDT 95 Crore to BDT 554 Crore. His foundation was built in FMCG field sales and modern trade, where he was named Best Territory Officer of the Year nationwide.
